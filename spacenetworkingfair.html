    SEDS Space Networking Fair

    What is Space Networking Fair

    Space Networking is held by TAMU SEDS every Spring. The goal of Space Networking is to connect students with the larger space community at Texas A&M. The Space Networking Fair aims to also connect student organizations with students to demonstrate their projects, showcase their work, and connect them with companies and university affiliates.
