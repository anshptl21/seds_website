    What is Student Astronaut Corps

    Student Astronaut Corps offers the unique opportunity for students to engage with other passionate students to learn all about space. We teach students spaceflight, bioastronautics, space medicine, rocket design, and much more. In addition to lectures, we provide workshops for students to learn new skills such as coding, modeling, or simulations

    Lectures, workshops, group trips, and so much more.

    Join the Student Astronaut Corps if you want to learn everything there is about space, gain technical skills to build your own model rocket, or design your own fighter jet. Join us on group trips to labs and the Johnson Space Center. There is always something new going on with SAC, come join us for a meeting every Wedensday 7-8 PM.
