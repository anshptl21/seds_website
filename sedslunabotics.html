    SEDS Lunabotics

    Howdy! We are TAMU Lunabotics, a student team run by TAMU SEDS in collaboration with Aggies Create - designing, building, and testing lunar construction robots for the annual NASA Lunabotics competition. Our mission is to develop the next generation of engineers by providing an intensive, hands-on experience centered around extraterrestrial robotics and NASA systems engineering principles, all while contributing to a sustainable human presence on the Moon.

    Our Beginnings

    When this team was founded in September 2024, we had to start from scratch, with zero prior funding or experience. And yet, in our first year, we raised over $12k, built a working robot, and submitted our NASA deliverables on schedule. Out of 68 initial applicants, we were one of 35 teams that made it to the on-site competition in Florida, where we managed to come back from a disappointing first run to place 21st overall. We were also recognized as winners of the Nova Award for Stellar Systems Engineering by a First-Year Team, and were among only 11 teams to successfully operate their robot autonomously.
